Let's start with your resume

Job Desk needs one thing to get going: your resume. Everything the app does is built from it — the documents it generates, the letters it writes, the details it types into company forms, and the words it searches the job sites for.

Bring your resume in

Choose the file and Job Desk reads it. PDF (.pdf), Word (.docx), plain text and Markdown all work.

A PDF that is a scan or a photograph of a printed page has no text in it to read, only a picture of one. If yours is like that, paste the text instead.

Paste the text instead

Open your resume, select everything, copy it, and paste it here. This works for any file at all.

What happens next

Your details are read out and shown to you to check
Search words are worked out from your own experience
The board fills with roles that match them
Apply — CV, letter and email are generated for you

Your resume is read on this computer. Nothing leaves it except job searches and the emails you choose to send.

Does this look right?

Read from your resume. Anything wrong can be corrected here, and all of it can be changed later in Settings.

What it will search for

Worked out from your job titles and skills. You get full control of this list in Keywords afterwards.

One optional extra

Job Desk is fully set up and completely usable right now. This one thing is optional, costs money, and you can add it later just as easily.

An AI key changes three things:

  • Your CV is rewritten for each role — reordered and reworded into the employer's own language, instead of the same CV going to everyone. Your dates, employers and qualifications stay locked and cannot be changed.
  • The email is written from the job description — specific to that role, rather than a template with one sentence for you to fill in.
  • An honest read on whether you qualify — what matches, what is missing, and anything that blocks it, like a licence you do not hold. Keyword matching cannot see any of that.

Get a key — or skip, and add one from Settings whenever you feel like it.

Without a key you still get

Finding, ranking and filtering every role
Your CV as a properly laid out PDF and Word file
Applying by email, with a template letter
Company forms filled in from your details
Tracking, follow-ups and reminders

That is a perfectly respectable way to apply, and plenty of people apply exactly like this. Nothing is crippled or time-limited without a key.
